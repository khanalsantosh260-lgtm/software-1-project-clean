# Forest Adventure

Author: Santosh Khanal

## Game idea and objective

Forest Adventure is a command-line game about a village with a
polluted river. The player explores the Forest, Cave, and Castle
to help restore clean water.

## How to run and play

Run Project5_File_Handling/project/main.py using Python 3 or PyCharm.
No external packages are needed.

Enter no to start a new game and provide your name and age.
Use the numbered menu to move, collect items, and perform actions.

Choose 4 to check your inventory and progress.
Choose 5 to save and type lopeta to quit.
To load a save, restart, enter yes, and use the same player name.

## Three ways to win

1. Repair: Collect Tools in the Forest and the Filter part in the
   Cave. Go to the Castle and choose 6.

2. Cleanup: Choose 7 in all three locations, then choose 8 at
   the Castle to dispose of the rubbish.

3. Community: Choose 9 in the Forest to collect evidence, then
   choose 10 at both the Cave and Castle to get villagers' support.

Completing any one route wins the game. There is no losing condition.

## Sustainable development

The game connects to SDG 6: Clean Water and Sanitation through
equipment repair, responsible rubbish disposal, and community action.
Water restoration is simplified for gameplay.
